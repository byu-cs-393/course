# Topic Exam (Performance) — Sorting, Two-Pointer, etc.

> **Practice this question ahead of time**, then perform it live for a TA.

## The question

**[Design Twitter](https://leetcode.com/problems/design-twitter/)**

Design a simplified Twitter supporting `postTweet`, `follow`, `unfollow`, and a
`getNewsFeed` that returns the 10 most recent tweets from a user and everyone they follow.
Exercises merging ordered streams with a **heap / priority queue** plus hash maps for the
follow graph.

## How it works

1. Practice the question above ahead of time.
2. Perform it for a TA or the instructor **in 15 minutes or less** (pass/fail).
3. Report your result in Canvas using the template on the assignment.

## Learning objectives

- Quickly write correct, non-trivial code in a **proctored setting with no assistance** — no lookups, AI, or help.
- Build the fluency to perform under time pressure, so in a real interview your energy goes to **problem-solving**, not mechanics.
- Internalize the basic **syntax** of your chosen language until it is automatic.
